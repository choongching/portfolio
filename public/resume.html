About

12 years of product design, from banking apps and streaming platforms to the complex enterprise tools I work on now. At Trustana, that meant building the PIM and AI enrichment platform from zero. Today it processes 30K+ products a month for retailers across APAC and the Middle East.

I'm a hands-on IC. In practice, that means I'm the one reviewing session recordings to understand where users get stuck, sketching out different workflow approaches, exploring how complex operations can feel simple for people who aren't technical, and then building it out in the actual codebase. Not just Figma mockups. I prototype with real data and ship production-ready work.

Figma and Claude Code are what I use every day. Before Trustana, I was at Saleswhale designing AI conversational tools, Rakuten Viki working on streaming and community features including fan channels, subtitling tools, and a platform redesign for 40M viewers, and Aleph-Labs shipping digital banking apps for OCBC.
